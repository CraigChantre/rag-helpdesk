Title: VPN Troubleshooting

If your VPN is not connecting, follow these steps:

1. Check that your internet connection is working.
2. Verify your username and password are correct.
3. Restart the VPN client.
4. Try connecting to a different VPN server.
5. Disable any firewall or antivirus temporarily to test.

If the issue persists, reinstall the VPN client or contact IT support.
